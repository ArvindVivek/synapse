# Synapse Support

Need help? Send us a message through [our contact page](https://kitchenlabs-one.vercel.app/contact). We usually reply within two days.

## Common questions

**How does a draft work?**
Each team bans five champions and picks five, taking turns in the pro order: three bans each, three
picks each, two more bans each, then two more picks each. Blue side bans and picks first; red side
gets the last pick. Tap a champion (or a suggestion), then tap **Ban** or **Lock in** to confirm.

**Who am I drafting against?**
A practice opponent that plays by itself. If you chose a sample team to scout, it plays like them:
its players reach for their favourite champions. With **No scouting**, it plays the strongest picks.

**Are the teams and stats real?**
No. Synapse's original pro match database is gone, so the teams and players are made up and champion
numbers are estimates. Treat the advice as practice guidance.

**What do the win chance numbers mean?**
Your chance to win from the draft alone. The breakdown shows where it comes from, in points of win
chance: champion strength, teamwork, head-to-head matchups, and blue side's first-pick edge.

**What are Tournament, Fearless and Scrim?**
Tournament is the standard draft. Fearless is a series: champions picked in one game are locked for
the rest of it (tap **Play game 2** when a draft ends). Scrim lets you **Undo** your last move.

**Why does my report say it was written from the draft numbers?**
The AI writer was unavailable, or you asked for several reports in a row. You still get a full
report built from the same numbers; try again later for an AI-written one.

**Where are my drafts saved? Can I open them on another device?**
Only in the browser you played them in. A draft link opened elsewhere offers to start a fresh draft
with that link. Clearing your browser's site data deletes saved drafts.

**Is Synapse made by Riot Games?**
No. Synapse was created under Riot Games' "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy using assets owned by Riot
Games. Riot Games does not endorse or sponsor this project.
